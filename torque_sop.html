Threaded Fastener Torque Application SOP

Mission Critical Program
Color Key Potential Safety Hazard: Physical movement or location change: Informational sections:
Section 1: Pre-Work / Initial Conditions
1.
Ensure all PPE is being worn and is serviceable throughout entire process.
2.
Follow LOTO procedure to ensure equipment is de-energized using a Live Dead Live method.
3.
Prepare work site by removing all debris, tools and nonessential personnel. If unsafe conditions are present and unavoidable move to Backout procedure immediately.
4.
Identify fasteners to be torqued by clicking this link. (Hyperlink to torque layout page)
5.
Remove 3/8” drive Torque wrench from storage box on cart of gang box and verify calibration date is not past due. If date has passed move to backout procedure immediately.
6.
Select 6-point socket that corresponds to the fastener head size to be torqued from tool cart or gang box. Thread stick-out should be accounted for in this selection.
7.
Select boxed end wrench from tool cart or gang box preferably a double offset 6-point that corresponds to the fastener head that torque is going to be applied to.
Section 2: Verification of Torque Specification and Tool Functionality
1.
Determine appropriate torque spec per manufacturer documentation. If torque value is not provided in submittal, apply torque value according to ANSI/NETA ATS-2025 criteria found in tables 100.12. ANSI+NETA+ATS-2025.pdf
2.
Use hand to place selected socket on to nut side of fastener assembly to verify correct fitment. At this time ensure thread stick-out will not interfere with socket internally while torquing. Verify appropriate size with selected boxed end wrench as well as clearance for the handle of the tool.
3.
Move the selector switch to the “ON” position on the ratcheting head of the torque wrench. If not marked, the selector bar should point away from you and to the right.
4.
Apply the socket to the corresponding 3/8” square drive of the torque wrench and apply rotational force clockwise while listening for “clicks” to test the ratchet mechanism. Reverse rotational force to verify the stop works correctly and does not slip.
5.
Use the dial or digital torque setting function on the torque wrench handle to set the appropriate torque value.
Section 3: Apply Tools and Begin Torque Procedure
1.
Tighten the fastener assembly to the point at which all components are touching by hand.
2.
Apply the socket on the torque wrench to the nut side of a pass-through bolt assembly, apply to the bolt head of a blind hole or captive nut fastener.
3.
Place selected boxed end wrench on the opposite end of the fastener in the most mechanically advantageous position possible if applicable.
4.
Use the ratcheting head of the wrench to gain mechanical advantage when available for smooth application of force at a 90-degree angle to the fastener.
5.
Place hand on knurled or rubber section of the torque wrench and place opposite hand on boxed end wrench or use as a force balancer if applicable. If fastener breaks, moves improperly while torquing, has a thread deformation or any other failure move to Backout procedure immediately.
6.
Slowly and steadily pull torque wrench in a straight line at a 90-degree angle to the fastener and release pressure immediately after the single audible “Click” to ensure accurate torque.
Section 4: Verify Torque Application and Apply Tamper Seal
1.
Slowly and steadily pull torque wrench in a straight line at a 90-degree angle to the fastener and release pressure immediately after the single audible “Click” to ensure accurate torque was applied the first time.
2.
Remove the boxed end wrench and torque wrench from fastener. Set boxed end wrench in a safe location.
3.
Visually check the torque wrench setting to ensure it matches the prescribed value, set in Section 2 Step 5. If the torque setting has changed move to backout procedure immediately.
Validation
1.
Inspect mated surfaces for gaps, cracks, or deformations using a flashlight, mirror, or direct vision to ensure assembly integrity. If any of these or any other failures are noted move to backout procedure immediately.
2.
Notify Foreman, Engineer or Manager that torque sequence has taken place and is complete.
3.
Foreman, Engineer or Manager perform 10 percent of total fastener in panel spot check with minimum of 2 torque verifications starting at Section 3 Step 2 through Section 4 Step 3.
4.
Foreman, Manager or Engineer apply a different color torque tamper paint to signify the completion of the spot check process. Apply torque tamper paint in a single line to immobile surface, washers, nut, bolt threads, chamfers, and bolt points as a visual indicator of completed torque procedure as well as a fastener movement indicator.
5.
Apply torque tamper paint in a single line to immobile surface, washers, nut, bolt threads, chamfers, and bolt points as a visual indicator of completed torque procedure as well as a fastener movement indicator. Application recommendation can be found in ANSI+NETA+ATS-2025.pdf
6.
Replace all covers and clean up any out of place torque paint utilizing the prescribed method.
Backout
1.
STOP WORK
2.
PLACE equipment in SAFE condition
3.
COORDINATE with appropriate Contacts to determine safe path forward Foreman, Manager or Engineer.
4.
Once issue is resolved, return to appropriate Section and Step in this procedure, verify steps previously completed were unaffected and resume work.
