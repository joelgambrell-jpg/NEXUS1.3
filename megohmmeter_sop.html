Section 1: Pre-Work / Initial Conditions
1.
Ensure all PPE is being worn and is serviceable throughout entire process.
2.
Conform with Foreman in area that all wires are installed as specified on the drawing and ready for termination in space to be tested.
3.
Follow LOTO procedure to ensure equipment is de-energized.
4.
Prepare work site by removing all debris, tools and nonessential personnel. If unsafe conditions are present and unavoidable move to Backout procedure immediately.
5.
Retrieve Fluke 1503 or 1507 from tool cart or gang box and test battery using method on page 18 of the user manual.
https://www.fluke-direct.com/pdfs/cache/www.fluke-direct.com/1503/manual/1503-manual.pdf
6.
Ensure no part of your body is in contact with any exposed conductor including ends of test leads through skin, clothes or gloves to prevent electrical shock.
7.
Insert black test lead into common input terminal and insert black test lead to Insulation test terminal. Turn dial to 1000V setting and touch uninsulated tips of test leads together and press “TEST”. If “0” and 1050V is not shown immediately on screen move to Backout Procedure.
8.
Retrieve plastic caps from cart and place over exposed conductor ends opposite of test area preferably downstream secure using electrical tape.
9.
Secure opposite termination area using LOTO tools or a second trained person to ensure the conductors are not touched during testing procedure.
Section 2: Testing the Conductors
1.
Visually inspect the visible portions of the conductors to be tested for damage to insulation including but not limited to cuts, abrasion, wear or burns.
2.
Ensure no part of your body is in contact with any exposed conductor including points of test leads through skin, clothes or gloves to prevent electrical shock.
3.
Place the Common test lead onto the exposed portion of the phase 1 or A brown or black conductor and the insulation test terminal to the phase 2 or B orange or red conductor. Color designations correspond to system voltage ratings and can indicate an issue in construction of the equipment.
4.
Press the test button on the lead is equipped or on the meter then proceed as prescribed below while documenting each reading. If the shown reading is different move to Backout Procedure.
5.
Place the Black test lead on the 1 or A phase conductor and the Red test lead on the 2 or B phase conductor. Press the TEST button until the screen displays 11MΩ and 1050VDC then proceed as prescribed below while documenting each reading. If any conductor does not produce a test value of 11MΩ move to Backout Procedure.

Validation
1.
Verify all conductors to be tested have a passing value of 11MΩ associated with them recorded in documentation sheet.
2.
Notify construction Foreman that testing has concluded, and all conductors tested have passed.
